THE GAME

Build high. Know when to stop.

盖得高，更要懂得收手。

BuffNook Tower is a precision stacking toy: twelve blocks, real rigid-body physics and five unpredictable buffs. Drop, balance, then bank your height before the tower gives up.

BuffNook Tower 是一款讲究手感的物理堆塔小游戏：12 块积木、真实的刚体物理、5 种不可预测的 Buff。瞄准、落下、找平衡，然后在塔倒之前封顶结算。

01

Aim & drop

瞄准，落下

Drag anywhere on the bench to move the block. Tap the orange key or press Space to release it. Rotate in 15° steps with ↺ ↻, Q or E. Every block lands on a seesaw beam: weight off the pivot tips it, so spread blocks left and right and keep counterweighting to hold it level.

在台面上拖动即可移动积木，点橙色键帽或按空格落下。用 ↺ ↻、Q / E 以 15° 为单位旋转。积木落在跷跷板横梁上：偏离支点就会倾斜，把积木分散摆在左右两侧不断配重，才能保持横梁水平。

02

Bank the height

及时封顶

After three blocks you may bank it. The tower must stay still for 1.5 seconds. If it collapses while confirming you keep 50%; a collapse before banking scores 40%.

放下 3 块后即可封顶，塔需静止 1.5 秒。确认期间坠塌按 50% 计分，未封顶直接坠塌按 40% 计分。

03

Daily & sandbox

每日与沙盒

The daily run gives everyone the same 12 blocks, reset at 00:00 UTC. Your first finished run is your official score; replays are practice. Sandbox has no block limit.

每日挑战全球同图，UTC 00:00 刷新。当天第一把完成的对局记为正式分，之后重玩为练习局。沙盒模式不限块数。

BUFF CODEX

Five physics buffs. Three help, two hurt.

五种物理 Buff，三种帮忙，两种捣乱。

Buffs arrive attached to a block: the first block is always plain, then every second block carries one — six per run. Read the preview card before you commit.

Buff 会附着在积木上：第 1 块永远是普通积木，之后每隔一块必带 Buff——一局共 6 个。落手前先看左上角预览卡，同一块积木可能救塔，也可能终结本局。

G

强力胶

落地立刻焊死下方积木，并在接触点画出金色发光焊点。连接永不脱开，适合处理难放的悬挑。

FAQ

Good to know

常见问题

When does the daily reset?每日挑战什么时候刷新？

Every day at 00:00 UTC. The main menu shows a countdown and your local reset time. Everyone gets the same twelve blocks and buffs for that UTC date.

每天 UTC 00:00 刷新，主菜单会显示倒计时和对应的本地时间。同一 UTC 日期内，所有玩家的 12 块积木与 Buff 完全一致。

Why did my run score less than its height?为什么得分比塔高少？

Banking while stable scores 100% of the height. A collapse during the 1.5-second confirmation keeps 50%. Falling before you bank keeps 40% of the last stable height.

稳定封顶得 100% 高度分；1.5 秒确认期间坠塌保留 50%；未封顶坠塌只保留最后稳定高度的 40%。

What is the difference between official and practice?正式分和练习分有什么区别？

Only your first completed daily run counts as the official score. Later runs are practice: they are saved separately and never overwrite the official result.

当天第一把完成的每日对局记为正式分。之后重玩属于练习局，单独记录，不会覆盖正式成绩。

Is my progress stored online?进度存在云端吗？

No. Everything is stored locally in your browser. Clearing site data or using private mode will erase your bests and streak. Sound is off by default.

不。所有进度只保存在本机浏览器中。清除站点数据或使用无痕模式会丢失成绩与连签。游戏默认静音。
