What is it

BuffNook Tower is a physics game about knowing when to stop.

Twelve blocks land on a tilted seesaw. Some blocks glue, some sink, some float, some slide. Bank your height at any point to keep 100% of it — but if you wait too long, the tower collapses and your score drops to 40% of the height you reached. Free to play in your browser, no account needed.

How to play

Land blocks, keep balance, decide when to bank.

Keyboard Q · E · Space · L

Q / E rotates the falling block 15°. Space drops it. L banks the tower after 3 blocks — hold still for 1.5 seconds and keep 100% of your height.

Mouse Click · Drag

Click the left/right rotate buttons, tap the drop keycap, or drag directly on the canvas to aim. Everything works with just one hand.

Touch One hand, no scrolling

Tap rotate, tap drop, tap bank. On a phone the game fits the screen height automatically, so the canvas and the control buttons below it always stay visible — no scrolling, no pinching.

Goal & scoring

Your daily official run is locked on your first completed drop of the day (UTC reset). Any later run is a practice score that does not overwrite the official result. A successful bank scores 100% of your height; a collapse during the 1.5-second lock confirmation keeps 50%; collapsing before you bank keeps 40%. Banking at 300 cm scores 300 cm; collapsing at 300 cm scores 120 cm. Progress is saved locally in localStorage.

Buff codex

Five buffs, five different problems.

Buffs arrive attached to a block: the first block is plain, then every second block carries one — six per run. The preview card shows what is coming before you drop.

Tips that actually work

Three habits that win rounds consistently.

01

Wait a beat before aiming

The seesaw sways for half a second after each block lands. Aim when the tower settles, not when it's still wobbling.

02

Treat each buff as a question

Glue sinks and stays — good for anchoring the base. Lead presses the beam down — park it on the light side. Float cancels lead exactly. Ice slides everything — land it last or not at all. Expand grows after it lands — give it room or it will push the tower over.

03

Bank at your personal ceiling

Most players collapse around the 280–340 cm mark. If your tower hits 280 and nothing obvious is wrong, bank it — a bank keeps 100% of your height, while waiting too long drops you to 40%.

FAQ

Short answers, no fluff.

When does the daily challenge reset?

BuffNook Tower resets every day at 00:00 UTC. New seed, new sequence, everyone in the world sees the same 12 blocks that day.

Why is my score lower than the tower height?

Height is measured on the seesaw in centimeters — a successful bank scores 100% of your height, a collapse during lock confirmation keeps 50%, and collapsing before you bank keeps 40%. Banking at 300 cm scores 300 cm; collapsing at 300 cm scores 120 cm.

What is the difference between official and practice?

Your first completed run each day is your official score — locked, archived, never overwritten. Every run after that is a practice run, saved separately for your own benchmarking.

Is BuffNook Tower free to play?

Yes. BuffNook Tower is 100% free to play in your browser — no account, no downloads, and no paywalls required. Progress saves only to your browser's localStorage under the key buffnook.tower.save. Clearing site data will reset it.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On a phone the game fits the screen height automatically and the rotate, drop and lock buttons stay visible below the canvas — every action is reachable with one hand. Works on iOS Safari and Chrome on Android.
