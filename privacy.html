Privacy Policy

Privacy

We collect almost nothing. Here's what that means.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What happens on your device stays on your device

Game progress, scores, settings, and any other game state are stored in your browser's localStorage under keys prefixed with buffnook.. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly clear it. We do not have, and will never have, a server that stores your game data.

What we do not collect

No email addresses. No accounts, no newsletters, no password resets.
No names or usernames. We don't know who you are.
No precise location. No IP logging, no geolocation APIs.
No third-party trackers. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad networks.

What Cloudflare sees

BuffNook Tower is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process your IP address and user agent in the course of delivering pages and protecting against abuse. Their privacy policy applies to that processing. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise use that information.

Comments

If we add a comment feature in the future, it will be clearly labeled and will never require an account. We will never use comments to solicit personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the changelog below.
